"The Beautiful Germs" takes its name from The Blankness of Water, a poetry collection by Marin Sorescu. I love this title and the allure it carries—just as I do the girls around me whom I began photographing in 2014. It is their vitality and sorrow, their lives and imaginations, that have nurtured my way of observing and my style within the field of portrait photography. This project has been shot from 2014 to 2025 and may continue to be updated in the future.
