The title of the work is inspired by Gabriel García Márquez's novel "Chronicle of a Death Foretold."

After an accident, details are typically found in newspapers and online news.Photography, capturing "frozen moments," often reflects "a quietly vanishing past."

This series of works emphasizes, through narrative reporting, the disruption caused by images and events in the order of reality. Images precede events; they reveal accidents that have yet to happen or are currently occurring. From another perspective, it also hints at personal psychological horror and a struggle with self.
